About me

Hi! I'm Ángel, a full-stack developer from Cantabria, Spain, with over ten years of professional experience building software for consultancies and product companies.

Throughout my career I've had the chance to work across very different industries — from automotive and insurance to legal-tech and pharmaceuticals — which has taught me to quickly understand new business domains and focus on solving real problems rather than just writing code.

One thing that has remained constant is the trust people have placed in me. I've been fortunate enough to work more than once with former managers and employers, gradually taking on more ownership and becoming involved not only in development, but also in technical discussions, planning, code reviews and product decisions.

I still enjoy learning every day. Whether it's a new language, a different architecture or simply a better way to solve a problem, I believe there's always room to improve — and that's probably what I enjoy most about software development.
